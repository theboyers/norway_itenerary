Transportation 0/5

  • Book outbound flights PIT/MGW/IAD → OSL (1+ stops, 10-11+ hrs) ~$800-1,200/person
  • Book return flights OSL → US (11-13+ hours transit time)
  • Book rental cars (2 cars, Days 2-9) - pick up Day 2 morning & return Day 9 afternoon to save 2 days rental + Oslo parking
  • Confirm AutoPASS & FerryPay links rental vehicle tag for automated toll and Sognefjord ferry billing
  • International Driving Permit (IDP) - Obtain via AAA $30+ per driver

Lodging 0/4

  • Oslo hotel center (Days 1 & 9, 2 nights total) - Citybox $120 / Radisson RED $165 / Grand $320 per night
  • Flåm base lodging (Days 2-4, 3 nights) - Flåmsbrygga $235 / Flåm Marina $260 / Fretheim $310 per night
  • Loen fjord lodging (Days 5-6, 2 nights) - Hotel Loenfjord $257 / Hotel Alexandra $380 per night
  • Lillehammer / Sjusjøen base (Days 7-8, 2 nights) - Sjusjøen Cabins $150 / Scandic $220 / Arctic Dome $333 per night

Activities 0/13

  • Oslo Waterfront Sauna (Day 1 or 9) - Oslo Badstuforening public slots - BOOKS 2 WEEKS OUT
  • National Museum Oslo (Day 1) - $22 (220 NOK) adult entry, under 18 free
  • Borgund Stave Church (Day 2) - $15 (150 NOK) historical center admission
  • Flåm Railway & Zipline/Bike (Day 3) - ~$85 (900 NOK) round trip train - HIGH PRIORITY, book 2+ months ahead
  • Nærøyfjord UNESCO cruise (Day 3 or 4) - $64 (640 NOK) silent electric catamaran
  • Njord Kayak on Aurlandsfjord (Day 3) - $115 (1,235 NOK) guided stillwater tour
  • Aurlandsdalen Hike (Day 4) - Pre-book Aurland Taxi to Østerbø (public shuttle starts June 1) or hike Vassbygdi-Sinjarheim
  • Loen Skylift boarding pass (Day 5 or 6) - 595 NOK (~$56) adult round trip
  • Briksdal Glacier troll car (Day 6) - 300 NOK (~$31) round trip transport slots
  • Via Ferrata Loen Climb (Day 6) - ~1,790 NOK ($170) - Strict safety caps, book far ahead
  • Lom Stave Church (Day 7) - $12 (120 NOK) traditional entry pass
  • Sjoa River Rafting (Day 8) - Class I-II Family Rafting ($95) or Class II-IV Whitewater ($111)
  • Maihaugen, Fram, or Holmenkollen Museum (Days 8-9) - ~180 NOK ($18) entry options

Dining Reservations 0/6

  • Ægir BrewPub (Flåm, Days 2-4) - Timber hearth tables fill up, reserve early
  • Kest Tasting Room (Aurland, Day 3 Sunday) - 8-course regional menu (open Fri-Sun evenings)
  • Fretheim Hotel Arven Restaurant (Flåm, Day 4) - Sognefjord-focused upscale fare
  • Hotel Alexandra dinner buffet (Loen, Day 5 or 6) - Premier local seafood feast spread
  • LYNG mat & bar (Lillehammer, Day 7 or 8) - Highly-rated alpine seasonal kitchen, reserve
  • Smalhans or Den Glade Gris (Oslo, Day 1 or 9) - Essential city sharing plates / pork knuckle slots
